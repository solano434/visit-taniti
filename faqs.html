Before you go

FAQs

Get answers about payments, holidays, local services, and practical details.

What currency does Taniti use?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros, yen, or major credit cards. Banks offer currency exchange.

Are restaurants and attractions open on holidays?

Many restaurants and attractions close on national holidays. Check hours directly before visiting.

What voltage are the power outlets?

Outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Is English widely spoken?

Many younger residents speak fluent English. English is less widely spoken in rural areas.

What healthcare is available?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has multilingual employees.

What should I know about safety?

Violent crime is rare. Keep an eye on your belongings, especially in tourist areas, where petty theft has been reported.

What are the local alcohol rules?

The drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be sold or served from midnight to 9 a.m.
