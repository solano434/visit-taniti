Transportation

Find your way to Taniti, then choose how you’ll get around the island.

Getting to Taniti

By air

Most visitors arrive by air. The island’s small airport serves small jets and propeller planes.

Find rental cars near the airport ↗

By cruise ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week. Check your cruise itinerary for arrival details.

Getting Around Taniti

Rental Cars

A local rental agency is near the airport. Confirm vehicle availability and pickup details before you travel.

Buses and taxis

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. Private buses serve the rest of the island. Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Walking and bicycles

Taniti City is fairly flat, and Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot. Bikes and helmets are available to rent. Helmets are required.
